03 · Market Opportunity

Three demand streams, one channel

International tourism

2.39M arrivals 2024 (+14.7%); JKIA ~1.62M — 67.8% of Kenya's inbound tourists. JKIA+MIA hit 1,955,000 in 2025.

Domestic & diaspora

Nairobi's middle class, returning diaspora, weddings and events — the segment most incumbents ignore in marketing.

Corporate & institutional

NGO, corporate and event transport — served today by older corporate players, not consumer brands.

Buying behaviour has shifted: customers research on TikTok / Instagram / Google, convert on WhatsApp, and trust reviews and founders more than polished ads. The winners are not the biggest fleets — they are the easiest to find, trust and book. That is a digital problem, solvable in months.
